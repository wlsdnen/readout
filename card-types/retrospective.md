---
name: retrospective
status: trial
use_when: looking back over work that spanned many turns or sessions — what was built, the decisions taken, what is verified, what remains
blocks_required: [text, checklist]
blocks_optional: [metrics, timeline, gallery]
---
Before/after becomes start of the stretch vs. now.

- `text`: what was done, three lines at most, in the order it happened.
- `checklist` titled 주요 결정: each decision with the one reason or evidence that settled it.
- `metrics` without `before`: the size of what exists now (commits, tests, artifacts).
- `confidence`: one row per claim of "it works", each verified, estimated or unverified as actually checked.
- `next`: what remains, most blocking first.
